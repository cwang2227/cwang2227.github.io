Readings that made me think

Alessandro Ludovico & Florian Cramer

Post Digital Print

This book predicts that publishing will move towards a hybrid, conversational form known as "post digital print". Post digital print combines elements of current print distribution as limited edition and crowdsourced, with elements of digital print such as computer-processed information and the use of networked crowdfunding.

Would Ludovico consider the reader contributing to the final print a part of the future post digital print model? For example, providing the image and text files but allowing the reader to design their own layouts for printing.
