Readings that made me think

Bojana Coklyat & Shannon Finnegan

Alt-Text as Poetry

The reading talks about the importance of alt-text to improve accessibility for the visually impaired. The author advocates for the first priority of alt-text to be to describe the image as succinctly as possible and not to use it solely as a form of poetry.

Have designers/developers ever hidden easter eggs in alt-texts where additional information is revealed to those who use a screen reader?
