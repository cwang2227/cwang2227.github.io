Readings that made me think

Ben Duvall

New Modernism(s)

While modernism involved stripping the "signifier/signified" relationship to the bare minimum, post-modernism replaced that with a multitude of hyper linkage. Building off of that, hypermodernism seeks to provide some level of standardization to signing while allowing for varied content.

Given that memes and icons developed out of the assumption of a "superficial read" given the rapid scroll nature of the internet, I wonder how graphic design has further evolved with Tiktok and AI. How will graphic design transform even further as short form content and brain computer interfaces become ubiquitous?
