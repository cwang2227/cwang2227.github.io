Readings that made me think

Dot Tuer

Poetics of Absence

Frankel's Body Missing project stood out to me as a clever use of the medium of video and the web to record and provoke thinking around historical memory - or in this case, the absence of them.

In an age of increased surveillance and monitoring in part due to the advent of the internet and digital technologies, how might we observe the "poetics of absence" in our daily lives?
