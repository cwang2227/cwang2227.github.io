Readings that made me think

Paul Soulellis

Performing the Feed

Paul Soulellis compares the traditional notion of information dissemination, which is “known, traceable", with that of modern media, which consists of continuous feeds, enhanced surveillance, and interactions between the human and non-human.

Mirroring Paul's line of reasoning that the feed is continuous and limitless, will the future of technology (with VR and other immersive media) bring about a boundless and boundary-blurring simulation of reality? And maybe the more interesting question to ask is, who would want that reality and why?
