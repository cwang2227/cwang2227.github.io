Readings that made me think

Ramsey Nasser

Decolonising the Digital

I was really captured by this week's reading on decolonising programming languages/systems of representation in computers. I have actually wondered this exact question when I first picked up Python - can you program in languages other than English?

Just like how countries can choose to be open to international cooperation or isolationist, might there exist a future where countries revert to national standards of encoding languages such that computer systems are intentionally kept from communicating with each other globally?
