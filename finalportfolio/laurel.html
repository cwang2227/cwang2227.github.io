Readings that made me think

Laurel Schwulst & Édouard U

Be Mindful of Internet Use

Lauren's imagery of the website as a puddle stuck with me the most as it emphasizes the temporal nature of the website. Edouard U, on the other hand, appears to be getting at thinking of knowledge as a network of interconnected and interrelated sources/elements.

How can we balance subverting the “conventional look and feel” of a website when seeking a creative approach and ensuring the users of the website understand how to use/navigate it?
