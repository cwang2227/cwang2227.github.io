Readings that made me think

Ulises Carrión

Mail Art

I am intrigued by Ulises' distinction of "mail" from "art", with "mail" or the postal system serving as support for the dissemination and realization of art.

When we share art on social media platforms like Instagram or Tiktok, is this considered a form of "modern" mail art in that it is a form of mass dissemination that conforms to certain rules stated in the platform and that anyone is free to interact with or respond to it publicly?
